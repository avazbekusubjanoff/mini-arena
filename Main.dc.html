The arena

Built like a stadium. Sized like a club night.

Everything around the pitch is there for the match: light, seats, a place to change and somewhere to talk it over after.

Floodlights

Four masts, even light across the pitch. Evening games feel like match day.

Spectator stands

Tiered seating on every side, so friends and family get a proper view.

Changing rooms

Lockers and showers for both teams, kept ready between bookings.

Café corner

Hot drinks before kick-off and a table for the post-match debate.

Scoreboard

A live scoreboard and match clock, so every goal counts in front of the stands.
